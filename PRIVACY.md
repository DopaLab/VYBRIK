# VYBRIK privacy summary

VYBRIK stores countdowns, alarms, custom tabs, notes, repeat schedules, and imported alarm-audio references locally on the device. The app does not require an account. Android backup may copy this local data according to the user's system backup settings.

The Free edition contains Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. These services may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP-derived information, diagnostics, interactions, and consent choices to provide, measure, secure, and personalize or limit ads, depending on the user's region and choices. The Pro edition does not include these advertising or consent SDKs.

The Support VYBRIK button opens Ko-fi in the user's browser. VYBRIK does not receive payment details from Ko-fi.

Before publishing, replace this summary with a publicly hosted privacy policy that identifies the app publisher and support contact, describes retention and deletion, links to Google's and Ko-fi's applicable policies, and explains jurisdiction-specific privacy rights. The Google Play Data safety declarations must include data handled by every SDK in the edition being submitted.
